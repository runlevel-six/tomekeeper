Reading {{/* What it does and what it will not do, in the place where somebody is deciding whether to switch it on. The exclusions are the part worth stating: a reader who has starred something has said it matters, and scrolling past it is not a decision to be done with it. */}}

On the unread lists only. An article counts as read once it has been on screen for a moment and then scrolled off the top — a fast flick down the page marks nothing. Moving past a row with j counts too.

Starred and saved articles are never marked this way, and nothing else is touched: the archive keeps everything, and any row can be marked unread again with its own button.

Checking feeds {{/* What automatic actually does, because it is the default and nobody has been told: the interval is learned per feed, which is why a busy feed and a dormant one are not checked at the same rate. The reason to change it is usually one of the two ends — wanting everything sooner, or wanting a 70-feed list to stop making 70 requests an hour. */}}

Automatically means each feed keeps its own pace: checked more often while it is publishing, and progressively less often while it is quiet, between {{with .PollFloor}}{{.}}{{else}}the instance's floor{{end}} and a day. Choosing an interval holds every feed to it instead.

A single feed can be set apart from this on the Feeds page, and its own setting wins. {{with .PollFloor}}Nothing is checked more often than {{.}} whatever is chosen here — that limit belongs to the instance, not to the reader.{{end}} A failing feed is still backed off from until it recovers.
