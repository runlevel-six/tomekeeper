Reprocess your archive

Extraction runs again over the pages already stored, and produces whatever it would produce today: this build's extraction, at version {{.Version}}, and your own rules as they now stand. Nothing is fetched. Every input is already on disk, which is the whole point of keeping the page — an improvement reaches a decade of archive, including sites that no longer exist, without asking anybody's server for anything.
